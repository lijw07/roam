Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished, high-resolution landscape game UI art direction board for the existing Godot dungeon crawler ROAM. This is a concrete visual review of two menu screens, not a website or a marketing poster.
Input image 1 is a reference render of the game's ACTUAL existing six KayKit adventurers. Match their simple chunky low-poly toy-like proportions and cheerful expressive faces. The knight has silver armor and a red scarf, mage purple hat, ranger brown hair and blue scarf, rogues green clothing. Do not change this into realistic or dark gritty fantasy.
Board composition: two large legible 16:10 screen mockups side by side, left "MAIN MENU", right "CHARACTER SELECT", with a small restrained caption strip below each. Render at high resolution (ideally 3072x1536). Fill the board with the screen designs, little wasted margin.
Art direction: chunky beveled slate stone, selective worn antique brass corners, deep midnight teal inset surfaces, warm ivory text, tiny turquoise rune accents, warm amber torchlight. Simple thick geometric shapes and restrained detail harmonize with the low-poly assets. Highly readable, generous spacing, no ornate filigree. A recurring angular dungeon arch + small diamond motif, not a compass.
LEFT screenshot: warm inviting stylized low-poly stone dungeon entry, flagstone floor, a torch each side, chunky blocks and wooden door framing a cool blue corridor. Centered ROAM wordmark in large softly beveled ivory stone letters, small turquoise diamond below. Centered vertical menu stack with exactly three readable labels "Play", "Settings", "Quit". Play is gold-edged teal with a little diamond pointer; secondary buttons darker blue slate. This UI retains the existing centered title and centered vertical buttons. No characters necessary here. Button text must dominate ornament. Background quieter and darker immediately behind labels.
RIGHT screenshot: header "Choose Your Character". Left compact vertical six-item class list with exactly "Knight", "Barbarian", "Mage", "Ranger", "Rogue", "Rogue Hooded"; Knight selected with warm brass outline and a diamond marker. Center a faithful image of the ACTUAL reference Knight on a simple circular beveled stone display plinth, full body, friendly low-poly proportions. Right small readable information plaque with "Knight" and "Swords, polearms and shields", plus a sword and shield symbol. Footer "Back" and primary "Continue". The selected knight has no arbitrary stat numbers, invented skill trees or currencies. Background softly lit dungeon alcove with simple stone framing, not clutter.
At bottom of entire board small label "ROAM / UI ART STUDY 01". This is review concept artwork, not final code. UI letters must be spelled correctly. Keep labels large enough to read. Avoid gothic horror, photorealism, gritty damage, pirate styling, wooden tavern signs, app dashboards, browser chrome, excessive glowing borders, logo watermarks.
